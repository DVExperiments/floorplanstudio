Getting Started

Four stages take a design from an empty grid to a shareable plan.

  1. 1

    Draw rooms

    Choose Rectangle and drag out a room, or use Custom room to click its corners one by one. Rooms that touch share a single wall. Add doors and windows by clicking near a wall. Use Precision drawing when you know exact sizes.

  2. 2

    Furnish and landscape

    In Room Builder, drag rooms, furniture and fixtures onto the plan. Furniture snaps to walls. In Site & Garden, draw lawns, decks, paving, pools and planting. Use Property & Site to trace your section boundary.

  3. 3

    Explore in 3D

    Switch the canvas to 3D to walk around a dollhouse model of every floor. Set the sun from your location and time of day, choose a visual style, add a roof, then use Render 4K image for a high-resolution picture.

  4. 4

    Save and share

    Your work saves to this browser automatically, and Ctrl+S saves straight away. Use Export for PNG, SVG or PDF drawings, Print layout for scaled A4 or A3 sheets, and Download JSON to back up a design or move it to another computer.

About

What it is for

Floorplan Studio is a concept design tool for homeowners, renovators and students. Use it to try layouts, compare room sizes, arrange furniture, plan outdoor spaces and explain an idea to family, a designer or a builder.

It covers 2D floor plans across multiple storeys, a 3D dollhouse with roofs and sun shadows, landscaping, a lighting plan, a heating and energy estimate, and printable presentation sheets.

How saving works

There is no account and no server. Projects are saved in your browser’s local storage, on this device, for this website address. They do not sync between computers or browsers.

Clearing site data, using private browsing or switching browsers can remove saved designs. Download a JSON backup of anything you want to keep, and import it to continue elsewhere.

Limits of the simulations

Heating results come from a simplified thermal model of walls, glazing, ventilation and sun. Lighting levels are approximate illuminance estimates. Both depend on assumptions and are for comparing options, not for sizing equipment.

Roofs, wall thicknesses and 3D geometry are visual approximations. Nothing here checks structural, electrical, fire or building-code requirements.

Concept design only. Drawings from Floorplan Studio are not construction documentation and are not suitable for building consent. Work with a licensed designer, architect or engineer before you build.

0° = north points up on the grid; 90° = north points right. Rotating north changes the home's real-world orientation without moving rooms.

Window-specific glazing, exterior shades and eaves can be changed by selecting a window. Solar position uses an approximate NZ solar-clock calculation; irradiance and shadows are clear-sky approximations adjusted by cloud cover, not observed weather.

Building & weather assumptions

Outdoor °C is the daily mean; the optional swing peaks around 3pm and is lowest around 3am. Heat pumps lose capacity and COP in colder air. Solar gain is calculated from window direction, sun altitude, glazing and simplified shading.

Shortcut guide?

Exact size: expand Precision Drawing, enter the dimensions, then click a start corner with Rectangle selected. Measure: press M and click two points. Layers: use visibility and lock controls to organise the plan. Multi-select: Drag a selection box from empty canvas, or Shift-click objects; drag any selected room to move the whole group. 4 opens Site & Garden.  Furniture and heater arrows rotate around their centre. Rooms rotate in quarter-turns, preserving wall-attached openings. Doors and windows stay attached to their walls; door arrows change their hinge or swing, and window arrows slide them along the wall. Heater direction is visual only and does not yet change simulated heat flow. Shortcuts are disabled while typing in fields.
